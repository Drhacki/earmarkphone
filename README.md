# Earmark for your phone

Learn from recorded lectures by listening, explaining and recalling out loud. This version runs entirely on your phone: your lectures and progress are kept on the phone, and Gemini, with your own key, transcribes lectures, picks chapters and terms, hears your spoken answers and checks your explanations. No PC or server is needed.

**Open it:** the address of this repository's GitHub Pages site, in Chrome on your phone. Then tap Chrome's **⋮** menu and choose **Install app**.

**First time:** in **Settings**, paste your Gemini key from aistudio.google.com and press **Test**. Then, in **Library**, choose a lecture recording (MP3 or M4A). Keep Earmark open while it's prepared; if it's interrupted, it carries on from where it stopped.

**Good to know:**
- Your lectures and progress live only in Chrome on this phone. Clearing Chrome's data for this site, or uninstalling the app, deletes them, so keep your original recordings.
- Listening and review work without internet. Transcribing and checking need it.
- What's sent, only to Gemini with your key: lecture audio in 3-minute pieces, transcripts, your spoken answers, and your explanations with the part of the lecture they're about. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what you send.
- Not in the phone version yet: turning PDFs and notes into lectures, and your references.

These files are made from the PC version of Earmark: `app.js` by its `build_phone_app.py`, from `web/app.js`.
